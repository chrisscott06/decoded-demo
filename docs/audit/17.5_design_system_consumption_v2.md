| Field | Before | After | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|

The brief author anticipated (a) but not (b).

This is **not a pipeline bug**. The math reconciles tautologically (the new portfolio value IS the sum of the per-site subfields). Surfacing here for Chris's awareness before Part 3 rewrites the chart narrative — the new prose will need to reflect that adding Sycous heat as a category is *adding visibility*, not artificially inflating the number.
