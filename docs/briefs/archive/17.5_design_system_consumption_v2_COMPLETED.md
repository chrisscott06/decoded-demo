## Why this brief

Brief 17 Part 2 shipped a functional Consumption tab but rendering review (Chris, 3 Jun) surfaced four problems that together demand a reset *before* Parts 3–5:

1. **Design system has fallen behind the content work.** Three nav ribbons stacked above the chart eat vertical space (primary nav, "Map · Energy", "Consumption · Heating · Power · Metering"). The page lacks the slim ribbon discipline and left-aligned grid the EOC report uses well — see EOC reference screenshots (in `docs/audit/17.5_references/`). Continuing to build Heating / Power / Metering on this template means four pages to unwind later, not one.
2. **Chart is too big and the controls are scattered.** Top-right call-out card, top-left toggle pills, bottom legend strip — the reader looks in three places to interrogate one chart. The fix is one consolidated control surface that *is* the legend *and* the filter.
3. **arbnco-derived resident is wrong at bulk-meter sites.** The current portfolio token `derived_resident_gwh: 5.62 GWh` includes arbnco-minus-Eco deltas at sites where there are no resident MPANs to derive from (Austin Heath, Gifford Lea, Millfield Green, Ampfield Meadows, Blendworth Hills, Millbrook Village). At those sites the delta is meter-count or timing artefact, not resident consumption. Resident energy at bulk-meter sites comes from Sycous (where covered), not arbnco. The strip-rule needs codifying in the pipeline and applied through the chart.
4. **`sites.json` correctness — Elderswell** has no `grid_type` set (`?` in pipeline output). Confirmed by Chris: Elderswell is **DNO (individual MPANs) for electricity** (residents on own MPANs — explains the arbnco delta) and **Sycous-covered for heat & hot water** (73,787 kWh measured). Sets cleanly as a DNO+Sycous hybrid like Ledian.

## The strip-rule, definitive

Resident energy is computed per site by arrangement, not portfolio-wide:

| Site | Arrangement | Resident-elec source | Resident-heat source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bramshott Place | DNO | arbnco-minus-Eco | — (residents on own gas) |
| Durrants Village | DNO | arbnco-minus-Eco | — |
| Great Alne Park | DNO | arbnco-minus-Eco | — |
| Elderswell | DNO + Sycous heat | arbnco-minus-Eco | Sycous (73,787 kWh) |
| Ledian Gardens | DNO-IVG-cable + Sycous heat | arbnco-minus-Eco | Sycous (274,390 kWh heat) |
| Austin Heath | Bulk microgrid + Sycous | **Sycous (233k kWh)** — strip arbnco | **Sycous (584k kWh)** |
| Gifford Lea | Bulk microgrid + Sycous | **Sycous (252k kWh)** — strip arbnco | **Sycous (688k kWh)** |
| Millfield Green | Bulk microgrid + Sycous | **Sycous (2.25M kWh)** — strip arbnco | — (GSHP, in elec figure) |
| Ampfield Meadows | Bulk microgrid + Sycous | **Sycous (165k kWh)** — strip arbnco | — (ASHP, in elec figure) |
| Blendworth Hills | Bulk microgrid + Sycous (zero reads) | strip arbnco; Sycous zero | strip arbnco; Sycous zero |
| Millbrook Village | Bulk microgrid, no Sycous | **strip arbnco; no resident segment** — label honestly | — |
| Sonning Common | Bulk microgrid, no Sycous, **out of GRESB FY25** | strip arbnco; no resident segment | — |
| Edwalton Office | NHH office, **out of GRESB FY25** | n/a (no residents) | n/a |

**Implementation:** the rule lives in `pipeline/build.py` (or appropriate readers module) as a per-site resolver. The resulting `resident_kwh_by_source` field per site has three subfields: `submetered_elec`, `submetered_heat`, `arbnco_derived_elec`. Only DNO sites populate `arbnco_derived_elec`; only Sycous sites populate the sub-metered fields. Sites with neither (Millbrook, Sonning Common bulk) get null subfields and are honestly labelled in the chart legend / tooltip.

**Portfolio token recompute:** `portfolio_inscope.derived_resident_gwh` becomes the sum of all three subfields across in-scope sites. **Expect the number to drop meaningfully from 5.62 GWh** — the bulk-site arbnco deltas previously inflating it are removed. The new figure is the right one; the current is wrong.

## Principles

1. **Design system is the contract.** Every change in Part 1 lands in tokens, not component-local CSS. Future thematic pages inherit by token, not by copying styles.
2. **One control surface per chart.** Legend and filter are the same thing. No call-out cards, no separate filter pills above, no separate legend below. One grid that does both.
3. **Strip-rule is structural, not cosmetic.** Bulk-meter sites do not get arbnco-derived resident segments. Sycous-where-available, honest gap where not. The portfolio total recomputes accordingly.
4. **GRESB-26 sites visible but muted by default.** Sonning Common and Edwalton render at lower opacity with a "GRESB 26" label. A single top-of-chart filter pill toggles their visibility entirely on / off. They never count toward in-scope totals regardless.
5. **CLAUDE.md Rule 10 (Recharts heights) applies.** Vertical bar chart still uses ResponsiveContainer; parent must have explicit pixel height. Falsifiability grep at close: `grep -rn 'height="100%"' eir/src/components/portfolio/Energy*` — every hit verified against resolved-height parent in walkthrough.
6. **Cream register, IVG fonts, Source Serif on site names only** (Brief 14). New palette tokens declared **only** if they don't exist; map view colours reused exactly.
7. **Browser-verify is the gate** (Hard Rule + Briefs 12–17 lesson). AFTER screenshots mandatory.
