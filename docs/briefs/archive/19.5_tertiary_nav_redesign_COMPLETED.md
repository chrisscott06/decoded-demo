## Why this brief

Tertiary nav review (Chris, 4 Jun) found the current pill-on-active treatment too noisy and visually indistinct from the secondary nav above it — three near-identical pill patterns descending in size means the user can't tell which level of the hierarchy they're on. Prototype review picked **Option A (underline only)** as the cleanest replacement: text-only inactive tabs, coral underline on active, no pill backgrounds.

Primary and secondary navs **stay as they are**. Only the tertiary changes. This gives the nav hierarchy three genuinely distinct visual weights, descending: filled pill → underline → text-only-with-active-underline.

**The critical thing this brief must NOT break:** the existing left-edge alignment grid. Primary nav buttons, secondary nav buttons, tertiary tabs, page title, body text — all currently align to the same left edge inside the centred 1280px container (per Rule 11 and Brief 17.5.3 Part 3). The new tertiary treatment must inherit this alignment without drift.

## What changes

**Tertiary nav visual treatment** on every thematic page (currently rendered on the Energy page; pattern propagates to Water / Waste / Carbon when they ship).

### Active state — current vs target

**Current (to be removed):**
- Active tab renders as a filled coral pill (`background-color: var(--coral)` or token equivalent)
- Inactive tabs render as a softer-coral pill or muted pill background
- Pill border-radius: ~999px (full pill shape)

**Target (Option A from prototype):**
- Active tab renders as **text only** with `color: var(--coral)` and a **2px coral underline below** the text (`border-bottom: 2px solid var(--coral)`)
- Inactive tabs render as **text only** with `color: var(--text-muted)` and `border-bottom: 2px solid transparent`
- No pill backgrounds anywhere on the tertiary nav
- On hover: `color: var(--text)` (slightly brightened), no underline change
- Spacing between tabs: 28px gap

### What stays unchanged

- Tab labels themselves (`Consumption · Heating strategy · Power strategy · Metering & data quality`)
- Primary nav (filled coral pill on active)
- Secondary nav (current treatment)
- Type token: tertiary nav uses `--text-body` (existing Rule 11 scale)
- Strip height: 32px (Rule 11 measured number, unchanged)
- The strip background itself (transparent, or whatever it currently is — don't redesign the band, only its contents)

## The alignment guard — non-negotiable

Brief 17.5.3 established that all four bands in the nav hierarchy share the same left edge inside the centred 1280px container:

- Primary nav left edge of leftmost content (e.g. "Home" button)
- Secondary nav left edge of leftmost content (e.g. "Map" button)
- Tertiary nav left edge of leftmost content (e.g. "Consumption" tab)
- Body content left edge

At 1920 viewport, this shared left edge sits at x ≈ 320px (i.e. (1920 - 1280) / 2 = 320 minimum margin, plus the page-edge-x padding inside the container).

At 1440 viewport, the shared left edge sits at x ≈ 80px.

**The new tertiary nav treatment MUST preserve this alignment.** Specifically:

- The strip background may span the full viewport edge-to-edge (with `background:` color or whatever current treatment is)
- The strip's **inner content** must sit inside a centred `<div>` with `max-width: var(--nav-content-max-width)` (token from Brief 17.5.3), `margin: 0 auto`, and `padding-left/right: var(--page-edge-x)`
- The first tab's left edge MUST match the primary nav's leftmost button left edge, the secondary nav's leftmost button left edge, and the body container's left edge — measurable via browser devtools, tolerance ±2px

**Falsifiability:** PASS criterion 6 below requires browser-devtools coordinates pasted for all four left edges at 1920 viewport. Numbers must match within 2px.

## Principles

1. **Alignment is the gate.** PASS criterion 6 is the hard test. If the left edges don't align within 2px, the brief hasn't passed regardless of other criteria being met.
2. **No new tokens.** Use existing `--coral`, `--text-muted`, `--text`, `--text-body` from Rule 11's type scale. The underline thickness (2px) is a hardcoded constant inline — not worth a token.
3. **Rule 11 layout numbers unchanged.** Strip height 32px, container 1280px, page-edge-x 32px minimum — all stay. Only the *active-state styling* on the tertiary tabs changes.
4. **No regression on other nav levels.** Primary and secondary navs render identically to before. Verifiable by screenshot comparison.
5. **Browser-verify is the gate** (Rule 9). 1920×1080 primary walkthrough, 1440×900 graceful scale-down. AFTER screenshots both viewports.
